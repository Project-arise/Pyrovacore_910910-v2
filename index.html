Who We Are

Built in India.
Built for Impact.

Pyrōva Robotics is an India-based social robotics startup on a mission to bring intelligent, expressive machines into everyday commercial spaces. We design and build custom social robots that greet, guide, entertain, and engage — turning ordinary venues into extraordinary experiences.

From a robot barista that takes orders to a fantasy store guide that tells lore — every Pyrōva bot is hand-crafted for your brand, your customers, and your story.

🚀 Founder & CEO

Metla Vishnu Vardhan founded Pyrōva Robotics with a vision to bring intelligent social robots and AI-powered experiences into businesses, events, and public spaces across India.
